SyncProgress Oct 01, 2019

Agile team management made easy.

2 Website

SyncProgress tracks your daily progress and helps you run your team meetings. Email & Slack reminders to make sure team members never forget to stay in sync.
SyncProgress integrates with Github to offer pull request reminders and extra team reporting. We also integrate with Trello to offer outstanding card reminders too.
SyncProgress is aimed at software team leaders.
